## Prompt 2: the brief

Goal: write docs/ui-brief.md from my answers, so every later step can point at one page instead of re-explaining.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Every screen has a signed-out behaviour; every chat state in the brief maps to something the agent emits
      (text, tool part, approval request, error, "ran out of steps", rate limit, reminder).
- [ ] Tokens have values, and the text colours pass 4.5:1 on the background.
- [ ] References are https URLs from the source you named, each with a reason.
- [ ] Reject: a brief that picks a framework or library you didn't (React, Tailwind, shadcn), or "modern and clean"
      with no values (P13: examples over adjectives).

## Three biggest gaps a coding agent would have to guess at

1. **Token values:** there is no accent colour, no background or text colours for light or dark, no spacing scale and no radius, so the agent picks the palette itself and can't confirm the 4.5:1 contrast it's told to meet.
2. **Approval lifecycle:** the brief doesn't say what the card shows after Approve or Deny (running, approved, denied, result), whether it guards against a double click, what the screen reader announces, or how approval works without JS.
3. **Navigation, sign-out and phone layout:** nothing says how you get between `/` and `/notes`, where sign-out lives, or how "works on a phone" looks (breakpoints, whether the message box sticks to the bottom, how the keyboard affects it).
